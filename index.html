Focus

AI systems that stay useful under pressure.

I am building toward AI research roles at the intersection of ML security, reinforcement-learning-style agent evaluation, and language technologies for real-world institutions. My strongest work asks how autonomous systems behave when instructions, incentives, context, and political stakes conflict.

The double major matters: CS gives me the tools to build and evaluate models; Global Studies gives me the habit of asking whose language, safety, records, borders, and institutions are represented in the data.

01 ML Security

Context-aware detection for indirect prompt injection in LLM agents, evaluated across AgentDojo, AgentDyn, and AutoDojo-style adaptive attacks.

02 Political NLP

Automated pipelines for 230+ interviews with Indian municipal officials, converting qualitative transcripts into structured language-use datasets.

03 Research Systems

Django, React, and database infrastructure for historical immigrant records, with ingestion, cleaning, search, and custom admin tools.

04 Global Fieldwork

Field-based learning in Mexico and Italy on migration, Indigenous sovereignty, extractivism, development policy, and international institutions. Read more.
