About Me

Hi! I'm Buzhao (Tavia) Liu. I work on making AI agents safe to deploy and useful in the physical world. I'm an undergraduate at Washington University in St. Louis studying computer science and international relations, where I work with Yevgeniy Vorobeychik on LLM agent security and with Sunita Parikh on computational social science.

To understand what I'm thinking about right now, read in order:

  • Context-aware detection of malicious instructions seen by LLM agents, submitted to AAAI 2027.
  • Self-evolving code policies, and where they break under stochastic dynamics.
  • A taxonomy of hybrid self-evolving agent systems.
  • A bimanual multi-agent robot learning benchmark built in ManiSkill.

Some other things worth mentioning:

  • I study how Indian municipal politicians change language between voters and government offices, using 230+ interviews.
  • I built a searchable archive of 1904 to 1940 Chinese immigration records from San Francisco passenger lists.
  • I have done fieldwork in Cuetzalan, Roma, and Guizhou on territorial defense, policy knowledge, and ethnic identity under modernization.

I think of myself as a global citizen in a practical way. Technology gives us speed, but humanity decides the direction. Feel free to reach out at buzhao@wustl.edu.
