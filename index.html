About Me

Hi, I'm Buzhao (Tavia) Liu. I study computer science and international relations at Washington University in St. Louis, and I build AI agents that can be trusted once they leave the lab.

With Yevgeniy Vorobeychik I work on security for LLM agents. My current project is a context-aware defense that catches malicious instructions hidden in the text an agent reads. In my own experiments I study self-evolving code policies, where a language model writes and rewrites the policy itself, and the multi-agent settings where that approach holds or breaks, from Overcooked to bimanual robots in ManiSkill.

The social science half of my degree is just as active. With Sunita Parikh I turned 230+ interviews with Indian municipal politicians into structured data on how their language shifts between voters and government offices. I have done fieldwork in Cuetzalan, Roma, and Guizhou, and I built a searchable archive of 1904 to 1940 Chinese immigration records from San Francisco passenger lists.

Technology gives us speed, but humanity decides the direction. That sentence is why I keep both halves of my degree. Write to me at buzhao@wustl.edu.
