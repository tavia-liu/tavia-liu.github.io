About

I am a senior at Washington University in St. Louis, majoring in Computer Science and Global Studies. I work with Prof. Chongjie Zhang in the Machine Intelligence Group and Prof. Yevgeniy Vorobeychik on LLM agent security. Previously, I worked with Prof. Sunita Parikh on language and power in Indian politics.

I am broadly interested in trustworthy and embodied AI. My research goal is to build intelligent agents that can learn, reason, and act reliably in the real world. My current work spans robotics, recursive self-improvement, LLM agent security, and multi-agent reinforcement learning.

I also see myself as a global citizen. Fieldwork in Mexico, Italy, and China has shaped how I think about AI across cultures, needs, and values. With this global perspective, I aim to push the capabilities of AI while making its behavior more reliable.
