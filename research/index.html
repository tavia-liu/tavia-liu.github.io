Robust Context-Aware Detection of Malicious Instructions in Text

Buzhao Liu, Xinhang Ma, Yevgeniy Vorobeychik

Submitted to AAAI 2027

We propose CAD, a query- and context-aware defense for detecting malicious instructions in text used by LLM agents. By classifying text at the sentence level and hardening the detector with feature-space and LLM-paraphrase adversarial training, CAD improves robustness against static and adaptive indirect prompt injection attacks while preserving agent utility across application domains.

Language Use in Indian Municipal Politics

Buzhao Liu, Sunita Parikh

working paper / computational social science study

We study how Indian municipal corporators choose language across campaigns, public meetings, constituent grievances, and bureaucratic work. Using 230+ interviews, I developed an AI-assisted pipeline that extracts language-use variables from qualitative transcripts and measures shifts between standard Hindi, regional dialects, and other local languages. The project shows that local political speech is not just communication. It is a practical way for politicians to adjust to audience, place, and institutional pressure.

Acting Out Who I Am: Will the Ethnic Identity of Minorities Die Out in Modernization?

Buzhao Liu

Young Scholars Academic Journal Volume 3, June 2022

We examine tourism-driven ethnicization in Guizhou and ask how modernization reshapes minority identity. Using ethnographic fieldwork and interviews with Buyi, Maonan, and Miao communities, the paper compares primordialist and constructivist explanations and shows that responses to ethnicization vary across groups and within the Miao, where leaders and residents negotiate culture, economic opportunity, and identity differently.
