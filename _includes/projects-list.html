National Archives Immigrant Database.

Buzhao Liu, with Prof. Steven B. Miles.

Humanities Digital Workshop, WashU, 2025.

website code

Chinese immigration records from 1904 to 1940 survive as scanned passenger lists that are hard to search. Working from 900+ digitized archival pages, I built a GPT-4-assisted OCR and entity-extraction pipeline, recovering 200+ Chinese migrant records with 30+ structured fields each from a noisy 56K-entry dataset, now searchable for historical research.
