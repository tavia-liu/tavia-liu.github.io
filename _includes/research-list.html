Robust Context-Aware Detection of Malicious Instructions in Text.

Buzhao Liu*, Xinhang Ma*, Yevgeniy Vorobeychik

Under review

paper code

LLM agents that read emails, web pages, and tool outputs can be hijacked by instructions hidden in that text. We introduce CAD, a detector that reads every sentence together with the user's query and its surrounding context, and harden it with adversarial training against paraphrasing attacks. CAD outperforms state-of-the-art defenses on AgentDojo and AgentDyn under both static and adaptive attacks while preserving the agent's utility.

Dialects of Dominance: Navigating Hindi Supremacy and Regional Language Dominance in Indian Municipal Politics.

Sunita Parikh, William O'Brochta, Buzhao Liu

In preparation

In India, the language a politician chooses is a statement about status. Drawing on interviews with 400+ municipal councilors across North India and Maharashtra, we combine computational sentiment analysis with structural thematic coding. Attachment to local languages is strong, but politicians shift to high-status standard forms to assert power, especially across gender and caste lines.

Scalable XAI Evaluation Using LLM-Simulated User Populations.

Buzhao Liu, Xinshi Feng; advised by Torrence Farmer, Chien-Ju Ho

Poster, 2025

poster

Evaluating AI explanations usually requires costly human studies. We test whether LLMs given real user profiles can stand in for human participants. Simulated users reproduce the direction of human behavior but exaggerate its size; asking them to consider cultural background raises their correlation with human survey responses across 92 countries from 0.59 to 0.81.

Acting Out Who I Am: Will the Ethnic Identity of Minorities Die Out in Modernization?

Buzhao Liu

Young Scholars Academic Journal, Volume 3, June 2022

paper

Does tourism erase minority identity? Based on fieldwork in Guizhou, I argue that modernization does not erase identity in one uniform way: how communities respond depends on who controls cultural display and who benefits from tourism.
