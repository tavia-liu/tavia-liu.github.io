This site is my public research notebook. I keep three kinds of writing here:

- **Research**: projects, paper notes, survey maps, and research questions.
- **Blog**: longer posts that have a clear argument or a more polished narrative.
- **TILs**: compact notes from experiments, literature review, debugging, and daily work.

The goal is to make the archive easy to maintain. A note starts as a Markdown file, stays private while it is rough, and becomes public when it is useful enough to share.

## Publishing Rule

Anything committed to a public GitHub Pages repository should be treated as public source. Private notes should stay in `_private/` or use the `.private.md` suffix so they remain local and ignored by Git.
